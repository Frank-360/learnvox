# LearnVox

> The world's most trusted AI-native tutor.

LearnVox is an AI-powered learning platform that transforms study materials into personalized learning experiences. Instead of simply summarizing documents, LearnVox teaches students using AI-generated lessons, quizzes, flashcards, audio explanations, and conversational tutoring.

## Vision

We believe every student should have access to a personal AI tutor that learns before it teaches.

Our mission is to build the world's most trusted AI-native tutor.

---

## Current Features

### 📄 Document Upload
Upload PDF learning materials.

### ⚡ Quick Learn
Generate concise, easy-to-understand summaries in seconds.

### 📚 Deep Dive
Receive comprehensive AI-generated lessons from uploaded content.

### 🎧 Audio Lessons
Convert lessons into natural-sounding audio for learning on the go.

### 🤖 AI Tutor
Ask questions about your uploaded document and receive grounded answers.

### 📝 AI Quiz
Generate quizzes automatically from learning materials.

### 🗂 Flashcards
Create AI-generated flashcards for revision.

### 💾 Save for Later
Download AI-generated study notes and audio lessons.
